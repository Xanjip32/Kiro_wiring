# KIRO student curriculum — draft outline

**Audience:** students who may be new to electronics and robotics.  
**Principle:** one topic, one diagram, one supervised exercise at a time.

| Lesson | Learn | Practice (low-voltage only) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Meet the robot | Identify arm, servo, controller, supply and wires | Label parts on a photo |
| 2. Power basics | Voltage, current, power and polarity | Read safe DC adapter labels |
| 3. Read a diagram | Supply, return, fuse and connector symbols | Trace a sample 5 V circuit |
| 4. Make a power budget | Add normal and peak load requirements | Fill in a simple worksheet |
| 5. Organize the cables | Wire labels, strain relief and service loops | Plan a paper harness layout |
| 6. Test systematically | Visual checks, continuity and voltage | Supervised low-voltage checks |
| 7. Build by subsystem | Bring one verified module online at a time | Observe a supervised demonstration |
| 8. Troubleshoot | Isolate causes and record measurements | Work through simulated faults |

**Teacher requirement:** mains wiring is never a student activity; hazardous motion systems require a reviewed safety procedure.

Each future lesson should include: **goal • components • wiring illustration • 3–5 steps • check • common mistakes**.
